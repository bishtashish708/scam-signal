# What to do now and where to report (U.S.-first)

## Always

- Don't reply, click, scan QR codes, or call numbers in the message.
- Verify independently: use the official app, or a number you look up yourself (back of your card, official website typed by hand).
- Never share one-time codes, passwords, PINs or your SSN with someone who contacted you.
- Screenshot the evidence, then block and report.

## If you already acted (lead with this when relevant)

| What happened | Do this now |
|---|---|
| Sent money by bank transfer / Zelle / wire | Call your bank's fraud line **immediately** (number on your card). Ask them to stop or recall the payment, and file a claim. Ask about a wire recall for wires. |
| Unauthorized transactions (you didn't make them) | Tell the bank/app they are **unauthorized** and that you're filing an error-resolution dispute; prompt reporting matters for your liability under Reg E. |
| Paid with gift cards | Contact the gift-card brand right away with the card numbers and receipt; keep the cards. |
| Sent crypto | Report to the exchange/ATM operator and to IC3 with wallet addresses and transaction IDs. |
| Entered card details on a site | Call the card issuer to cancel and reissue the card. |
| Gave a password or code | Change the password (and anywhere you reused it), turn on 2FA, sign out other sessions, and check for forwarding rules on email. |
| Gave remote access | Disconnect from the internet, uninstall the tool, run a security scan, change passwords from a different device, and call your bank. |
| Shared SSN / ID / DOB | IdentityTheft.gov recovery plan; free credit freeze at all three bureaus. |
| Lost a phone number (SIM swap) | Call your carrier; set a port-out PIN / number lock. |
| Someone offers to "recover" your money for a fee | That's a recovery scam. Don't pay. |

## Where to report

| Where | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| **FTC** | Any scam in the U.S. | https://reportfraud.ftc.gov |
| **FBI IC3** | Internet-enabled crime: phishing, crypto/investment, romance, extortion, BEC, large losses | https://www.ic3.gov |
| **Your bank / card issuer / payment app** | Any money or account involvement | Number on the back of your card or inside the official app. For Zelle, contact your bank. For Cash App/Venmo/PayPal, use in-app support only (fake "support numbers" are common). |
| **CFPB** | Your bank or payment app won't resolve a fraud/scam claim | https://www.consumerfinance.gov/complaint/ |
| **Carrier spam reporting** | Scam texts | Forward the text to **7726** (SPAM); then "Report Junk" and delete |
| **Phishing email** | Scam emails | Forward to **reportphishing@apwg.org**; use your mail app's "Report phishing" |
| **IRS / TIGTA** | IRS impersonation | Email: phishing@irs.gov · Calls: https://www.tigta.gov |
| **SSA OIG** | Social Security impersonation | https://oig.ssa.gov |
| **USPIS** | USPS-themed scams | https://www.uspis.gov/report |
| **FCC** | Robocalls, spoofed caller ID | https://consumercomplaints.fcc.gov |
| **IdentityTheft.gov** | Personal data exposed or misused | https://www.identitytheft.gov |
| **Elder Fraud Hotline** | Victim is 60+ | 1-833-FRAUD-11 (1-833-372-8311) |
| **NCMEC** | Sextortion involving a minor | https://report.cybertip.org |
| **The platform** | Marketplace, dating app, social DM | Report the profile/listing in the app |

Outside the U.S.: point to the national equivalent (e.g. UK: forward texts to 7726, emails to report@phishing.gov.uk; Canada: Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre). Say you're less certain of local specifics and suggest the user confirm.
